Inspiration

In online hackathons and university communities, people struggle to connect for real. Ice-breaker games are boring. We thought - why not connect through stories? Horror and suspense bring people together fastest. We added education to make it meaningful.

What it does

WhisperChain Connect is a real-time social storytelling platform:

1. User Profile Home Page:

  • Level system (Story Weaver Lvl 5), Badges (Connector, Suspense Master, Educator)
  • Stats: Stories Created, Real Connections Made, Edu Points
  • My Squad - online friends with invite

2. Game Page - 2 vs 2 / 4 vs 4:

  • 2 vs 2 Duo Duel (5 min): 2 players vs 2 players, fast suspense story
  • 4 vs 4 Squad Saga (10 min): 2 teams of 4, collaborative saga
  • Team Builder with Team Blue vs Team Red
  • Turn-based storytelling with 45s timer

3. Social Connectivity - Live Chatting:

  • Lobby Chat + In-Game Live Chat Panel
  • Quick reactions: 😱 🔥 💡 👏
  • After game: "You connected well, add to squad?"

4. Real Connect + Education:

  • Education Mode ON: AI gives challenges like "Use 'photosynthesis' in horror", "Add a historical fact from 1947"
  • Knowledge Meter + Suspense Meter
  • Learning Outcomes at end: 3 new vocab, 1 science fact used
  • Edu Points increase on profile

How we built it

  • Frontend: React + Tailwind CSS, Glassmorphism UI, Dark Theme #0B1220 with Teal #2DD4BF
  • AI Logic (Mock Gemini API): Keyword-based suspense twist engine - detects words like 'door', 'forest', 'phone' and gives relevant horror twist. 15+ pre-written twists.
  • Real-time: Firebase (planned) - currently mock local state for demo
  • Design: Discord + Wattpad inspired

Challenges we ran into

  • Making AI twists feel relevant, not random. Solved with keyword detection.
  • Balancing suspense and education - too much education kills fun.
  • Building 2vs2/4vs4 team logic in 2 days.

Accomplishments

  • Built full 4-tab app: Home, Play, Chat, Profile in 48 hours
  • Working live chat + story co-creation + AI twist
  • Unique mix of Communication + Gaming + Education

What we learned

Real connection happens when you create together, not just chat. Stories are the best ice-breaker.
